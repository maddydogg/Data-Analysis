## Description

The full moon was out. 6,000 visitors in pointed hats. One of them is a killer.

Every 31 October the village of Morrowmere holds its Moon Fair on the green. The village coven of herbalists, candle-makers and tea-leaf readers lights a lantern over every stall, and the cauldrons of spiced brew bubble until late. This year the shop boy at the apothecary found his master slumped in the back room at 20:45, his cup of brew laced with something from the locked cabinet. A lantern girl saw a visitor with a silver crescent pin on a tall pointed hat. Nobody saw a face.

The fair committee has handed over the complete Visitor Log: 6,000 moon tokens, one line each. Your job is to cross out every visitor who doesn't fit the evidence until only one remains.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- The case and the house rules (every term defined, so no clue has two meanings)
- 6 case documents: fair map, stall directory, the Book of Brews, the moon-watcher's log, a tea-leaf reader's reading book with a witness note, and the apothecary's ledger with the village carrier's rounds
- The Inspector's Notebook: 12 clues stated outright and 6 pieces of evidence hidden in the documents
- The Visitor Log: 6,000 suspects in hourly chapters
- 3 levels of hints for every clue, each level on its own pages, so you only see what you ask for
- The Sealed Check: confirm your answer without seeing any names
- A separate solution file (in a ZIP, so you can't open it by accident): the full story, a step-by-step solution and an index of all 6,000 tickets

HOW IT PLAYS
- 1–4 players, about 90–150 minutes
- Print it (US Letter or A4) or play it on an iPad in Goodnotes or Notability, with a clickable contents page
- Spooky, not gory: a cozy witch-village mystery for adults and teens. The witches are ordinary villagers; nothing in it makes fun of real beliefs.
