## Description

A murder mystery in 24 windows: open one each day from 1 to 24 December, and on Christmas Eve one name will be left.

It is Christmas Eve in the snowy city of Vellmouth. Quillon & Daughters, the grandest department store on Lantern Street, has unveiled one new Christmas window every evening of Advent. Tonight is Lantern Night: the band plays on the steps, the store stays open late, and 2,400 shoppers come through the doors with numbered Lantern Passes.

Ten minutes before the curtain rises on the Grand Window, the old window dresser is found in his workshop, slumped over his sketchbook beside a striped paper cup of cocoa. A few days earlier he had written to the police: someone with a little brass magpie brooch had been stealing from his windows.

The inspector sealed the evidence into 24 envelopes. Open one a day. By Christmas Eve, one Lantern Pass will be left.

★ HOW IT WORKS
• Day 1: the case, the window dresser’s letter, the store plan and the street plan
• Day 2: the Lantern Register: 2,400 shoppers, one line each
• Days 3–23: one new piece of evidence a day (a statement, a timetable, the city tram map, a store guide, a receipt…). Work out what it means and cross out every shopper who doesn’t fit
• Day 24: the Grand Window, the finale and the Sealed Check, which tells you if you are right without spoilers
• The solution is in its own file, so nothing is given away by accident

★ HOW TO PLAY EACH DAY
1. Open today’s window (an envelope on paper, or tap the day on the iPad calendar).
2. Read the evidence and the question at the bottom of the page.
3. Go through the shoppers who are still in on the register and cross out everyone who doesn’t fit.
4. Write down how many are left. On Days 6, 12 and 18 a check-in tells you the number to expect.
5. Stuck? Every window has three levels of hints at the back: a nudge, a stronger push, and nearly the answer.
The first days take 20–30 minutes (the register is long); later days take 5–10.
