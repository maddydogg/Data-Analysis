## Description

Two complete murder mystery puzzles. 12,000 suspects. Two killers. Tonight's programme:

FEATURE 1: STORM OVER CORVENMOOR
Monster Night at an old castle. In the middle of a thunderstorm the museum curator is found in the Laboratory Tower,
his goblet laced. A witness saw a visitor in a stitched monster mask slip out. Nobody saw a face.

FEATURE 2: FULL MOON OVER MORROWMERE
The Moon Fair in a cozy witch village. Under a full moon the apothecary's owner is found in his back room, his cup of
brew laced. A lantern girl saw a tall pointed hat with a silver crescent pin. Nobody saw a face.

In each case you get the full Visitor Log, 6,000 people, one line each. Cross out everyone who doesn't fit the
evidence until only one remains.

WHY THE DOUBLE FEATURE
- Two complete cases in one download, for less than buying them one by one
- Two evenings of play: one case per night, in any order
- 36 clues, 12 case documents and 108 hints in total
- One clickable programme page with every section of both cases, plus bookmarks for each case
- Each case has its own Sealed Check and its own sealed solution, so you can solve them separately

WHAT'S INSIDE (FOR EACH CASE)
- The case and the house rules (every term defined, so no clue has two meanings)
- 6 case documents. Storm: castle map, booth directory, receipt and witness note, weather log, night bus timetable,
  witness statements. Moon: fair map, stall directory, Book of Brews, moon-watcher's log, reading book with witness
  note, shop ledger with the carrier's rounds
- The Inspector's Notebook: 12 clues stated outright and 6 pieces of evidence hidden in the documents
- The Visitor Log: 6,000 suspects in hourly chapters
- 3 levels of hints for every clue, each level on its own pages
- The Sealed Check: confirm your answer without seeing any names
- A separate solution file (in the SOLUTIONS ZIP, so you can't open it by accident): the full story, a step-by-step
  solution and an index of all 6,000 tickets

HOW IT PLAYS
- 1–4 players, about 90–150 minutes per case
- Print it (US Letter or A4) or play it on an iPad in Goodnotes or Notability, with clickable contents and bookmarks
- Spooky, not gory: cozy mysteries for adults and teens

CHECKED BY CODE
A computer program checked each case before it reached you: there is exactly one answer in each case, every one of
the 18 clues is needed, and the answer stays the same even if a clue is read in a slightly different way.

YOU WILL RECEIVE
4 files: the double-feature case book as PDF for US Letter, A4 and iPad (both cases in each), plus the SOLUTIONS ZIP
with the solutions to both cases.

This is a digital download. Nothing will be shipped. For personal use: print as many copies as you need for your own
household or game night. Please don't share or resell the files.

All illustrations are drawn with code. No AI-generated images, no photos.
