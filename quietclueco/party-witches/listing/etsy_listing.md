## Description

A cozy, spooky murder mystery party for 6 to 12 guests, and the host can play too.

Every Halloween, when the Moon Fair in the village of Morrowmere packs away its last lantern, the Hearth Circle climbs the
hill to Larkwell Hall for the Lantern Supper: soup and cider by the fire, a midnight carol, and the lighting of the great
lantern in the tower. This year the Lantern Keeper promised an announcement at midnight. She never made it. At a quarter
to eleven she was found in the Still Room, a green glass of blackberry cordial empty beside her.

Every guest is a suspect. Nobody leaves until the Inspector knows who did it.

★ HOW MANY PLAYERS
• 6 to 12 players: 6 core roles that every game needs, plus up to 6 extra roles that join as your guest list grows
• The casting table tells you exactly which roles to use for 6, 7, 8… up to 12 guests
• The case can always be solved with the 6 core roles alone; the extra roles add witnesses, secrets and fun
• The host can play: no booklet says who did it, not even the killer’s, and the solution is sealed at the back of the host guide
• Bigger group? Pair guests up to share a role
• Teens and adults. Any guest can play any role

★ HOW THE EVENING GOES (2–3 hours)
1. Arrive: potions, name badges and a booklet in an envelope. Everyone reads who they are.
2. Prologue: the host reads how the Lantern Keeper was found.
3. Round 1: say who you are and where you were. Evidence cards 1–4 go on the table.
4. Round 2: secrets start to come out. Evidence cards 5–8.
5. Round 3: the last evidence. Cards 9–11.
6. Accusations: everyone writes who did it, how and why.
7. The reveal: the host opens the sealed solution and reads it aloud. Then the awards.
No acting needed: everything a guest has to say is in their booklet, in plain words.

★ WHAT’S IN THE KIT
• Host guide: preparation timeline, casting for 6–12 guests, a timetable for the evening, scripts to read aloud for every round, FAQ and the sealed solution
• 12 character booklets, 4 pages each: who you are, costume in two words, your secret, questions to ask, and a page for each round
• 11 evidence cards in 3 rounds, plus a plan of Larkwell Hall for the table
• Invitations: a fillable invitation, 12 fillable character invitations (who you play, what to wear) and phone versions to text
• Name badges (one for the host), detective’s notes and accusation sheets
• A potion menu: 4 drinks, each with an alcohol-free version
• 4 awards: Best Detective, Best Costume, Best Performance, Most Suspicious
• US Letter and A4

★ EXACTLY ONE ANSWER, CHECKED BY CODE
Every statement, card and timeline in this kit is checked by a computer program: one killer, every key piece of evidence needed, at
least two suspects left until the last round, and the same answer however a place word on the plan is read (next to,
across, through, above). The game works with any number of guests from 6 to 12.

★ GOOD TO KNOW
• Digital download: nothing will be shipped.
• Print at home in colour or black and white; inside pages are light to save ink.
• Spooky, not gory: candlelight, a bell and a poisoned cordial. No blood, no weapons.
• Set in Morrowmere, the witch village of our case file “Full Moon over Morrowmere”, with a new story: you don’t need to
  have played it, and this party gives nothing away about it.
• All illustrations are drawn with code. No AI-generated images, no photos of people.
• For personal use only. Please don’t share or resell the files.
