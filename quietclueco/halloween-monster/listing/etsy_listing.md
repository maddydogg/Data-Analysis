A storm over the castle. 6,000 visitors in monster masks. One of them is a killer.

Every 31 October the village of Corvenmoor opens its old castle for Monster Night. This year the lightning came right on cue, and at 20:50 the curator of the castle museum was found slumped over the Baron's workbench in the Laboratory Tower. A witness saw someone slip out of the tower in a stitched green monster mask. Nobody saw a face.

The castle ticket office has handed over the complete Visitor Log: 6,000 wristbands, one line each. Your job is to cross out every visitor who doesn't fit the evidence until only one remains.
