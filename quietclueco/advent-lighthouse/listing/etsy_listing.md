## Description

Christmas Eve on a tiny island. The lamp did not come on.

Candleholm is a small green island at the mouth of the Sound, with one white tower, one cottage, a boathouse and a keeper. That December, Ezra the keeper found the missing Christmas parcels for the outer isle hidden under a sail in his own boathouse, and began to write down everything he learned about the stranger in the red knitted cap. On Christmas Eve, at lighting-up time, Candleholm Light stayed dark.

The Keeper of Candleholm is a printable murder mystery advent calendar: open one window a day from 1 to 24 December, read one page of Ezra's journal and the paper he pinned to it, and cross out travellers in the Sound Book until one line is left on Christmas Eve.

HOW IT WORKS
- Day 1: the case, Ezra's first journal page, the lighthouse in section and a sea chart of the Sound.
- Day 2: the Sound Book, 2,400 travellers who crossed the Sound in December.
- Days 3 to 23: one handwritten journal page a day, with a tide table, an almanac, a code book of light signals, a waybill or a log pinned to it. Each one rules some travellers out.
- Day 24: Christmas Eve, and the Sealed Check that tells you if you are right without spoilers.

HOW TO PLAY EACH DAY
1. Open today's window.
2. Read Ezra's entry and the paper with it, and answer today's question.
3. Cross out every traveller in the Sound Book who doesn't fit.
4. Write down how many lines are left. On Days 6, 12 and 18, a check-in tells you the number to expect.
5. Stuck? Every window has three levels of hints at the back.
The first days take 20–30 minutes; later days take 5–10.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- US Letter PDF (90 pages) and A4 PDF (87 pages) to print
- iPad PDF (81 pages) for GoodNotes, Notability or any PDF app: the first page is a calendar of 24 tappable windows, and every window links back
- An envelope template and day numbers 1–24
- The solution in its own ZIP: the full story, the solution day by day and an index of all 2,400 lines

WHO IT'S FOR
- Couples: a December ritual with the evening tea
- Families with teens and grown-ups: split the Sound Book and race to the answer
- A gift for anyone who loves lighthouses, sea stories and cosy mysteries (instant download)
- 1 to 4 players

EXACTLY ONE ANSWER, CHECKED BY CODE
Every case is checked by a computer program: exactly one answer, every day needed, and the same answer whichever reasonable way you read the chart, the tide table and the almanac (through, higher up, near, between, rising, before, a.m. and p.m.).

GOOD TO KNOW
- Digital download: nothing is shipped.
- For personal use. You may print as many copies as your household needs.
- All illustrations are drawn with code in a vintage picture-book style. No AI-generated images.
- The solution is in a separate file, so nobody spoils it by accident.
